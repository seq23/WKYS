How do I create my next application?

Start with a written scope. Create a new private repository from your approved starter, open it in your development environment, implement a small feature on a branch, test locally, review the changes and deploy to staging before production.

How do I make a safe update?

Back up affected data, use a branch, run tests, review permissions and secrets, and obtain approval before releasing. Keep a rollback plan.

What if a system stops working?

Record the error without secrets, check service status and recent changes, follow your documented rollback procedure, and contact your designated technical support owner if needed.
