Privacy notice.

We Keep You Spry uses what you send through the inquiry form only to reply to you, assess fit and discuss a possible engagement. This page lists what the form collects, where it is stored, which analytics the site uses and how to ask for your information to be corrected or deleted.

The inquiry form

The form collects your name, email, company, goals and where you would like to start. Cloudflare processes submissions and Google stores them in a private Google Sheets file in our Google Drive. Do not submit confidential material or credentials.

The discovery quiz runs locally in your browser and does not transmit your answers.

Analytics

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. The home page and the pages that carry the inquiry form also load Microsoft Clarity, which records how visitors use those pages (clicks, scrolling and mouse movement) so we can improve them. Text typed into the inquiry form is masked and not recorded by Clarity. Clarity may set cookies; Microsoft processes that data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Clarity is not loaded on the client or founder sign-in pages or the portal demo.

Hosting services may process technical logs for security and operation.

Your information

To request access, correction or deletion of your inquiry information, contact hello@wekeepyouspry.com.
