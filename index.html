Questions, answered.

How much does an AI implementation consultant cost?

Our prices are fixed and published: the Assessment is , credited to the install; the 30-day install is ; the 90-day engagement starts . Software you choose is billed to your own accounts at its own price, and every cost is agreed in writing before work begins.

What happens in the 30 days?

Week one confirms the scope and sets up access in your accounts. Weeks two and three build, connect and test the agreed systems on real work. Week four trains your team and hands over every account, manual and recording. On day 30 your team runs it, not us.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. We tailor instruction to your current capabilities. Some advanced systems may require technical assistance beyond the engagement.

Will I own my systems?

Yes. We build in accounts in your name, and you keep control of your accounts, data and commissioned deliverables under your agreement. Nothing we build lives in our accounts. Third-party licenses and pre-existing We Keep You Spry intellectual property remain subject to their own terms.

Do you manage my business or generate sales?

No. We build and teach agreed technology systems. We do not manage sales pipelines, promise growth or operate your business.

What happens after the install or the 90 days?

You own and run what we built. You can stop there, request a separately scoped expansion with its own quote, or join an optional ongoing learning membership when available. There is no retainer that continues by default.
